In 1956, sitting on a café terrace in Amsterdam, Edsger Dijkstra worked out how to find the shortest route between two cities in about twenty minutes, without pencil and paper. His algorithm now runs inside map apps, network routers and video games.

But it has **two built-in limits**. It gives wrong answers when some edges have *negative* costs, and it can never be faster than *sorting*. For decades both looked permanent. Between 2022 and 2025, researchers broke through both.

This site tells that story, one live demo at a time.
